## From Google Rankings to AI Recommendations: What's Changing?

Today, customers can ask ChatGPT detailed questions or use Google AI search to find information, recommendations, and related sources. Instead of searching for *“digital marketing agency,”* one might ask:

> *“Where can I find an inexpensive marketing agency that knows small businesses?”*

That’s a more specific question and gets to what the customer really needs. 

AI-powered search experiences can summarize multi-source data and connect users directly to relevant sites. This creates more opportunity for businesses that have useful, accessible, and structured content.

### 2. Understand ChatGPT SEO, AEO, and GEO
Businesses had just gotten used to standard SEO, and now digital marketing has added more abbreviations! Thankfully, the ideas are simpler than their names suggest:

* **ChatGPT SEO:** A broad term that refers to practices that help businesses get found and referenced in ChatGPT search experiences.
* **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** The process of creating content that directly answers users’ specific, high-intent questions.
* **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Increasing the chances that your content is found, cited, or showcased in AI-generated responses.

It still requires clear content, credible information, useful explanations, and accessible web pages. There is no magic formula that will guarantee AI recommendations. ### 3. Build Credibility That Goes Beyond Your Website
Anybody can say, *“We’re the best in the industry.”* The other thing is to prove it.

* Share real customer reviews and transparent feedback.
* Publish helpful case studies, original insights, and authentic examples of your work.
* Clearly describe the sprint process and frameworks you follow.
* Don’t write fake reviews or exaggerate what you’ve done. 

Real credibility is earned through a consistent message and proven experience. Customers don't want the loudest business, after all—they are looking for one they can rely on. Our content marketing approach focuses on building this kind of authentic authority.

---

### 4. Keep Traditional SEO Strong While Adapting
The good news is that traditional SEO hasn’t packed up and retired! Technical website health, mobile usability, helpful content, and logical navigation are still critically important.

* Make it easy for search engines to crawl and index key pages.
* Ensure your website's `robots.txt` makes your content accessible to the OpenAI search crawler (`OAI-SearchBot`, `GPTBot`).
* Include AI search optimization as an integrated layer in your overall digital marketing strategy.
